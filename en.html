Recommended Itineraries

Depart from New Chitose Airport (delivery, approx. 35 min) or Shin-Sapporo Station (free pickup). All routes return to our office. Four popular ways to roam Hokkaido.

1
Furano & Biei
Lavender Route
🗓 3 days / 2 nights ・ best in Jun–Aug
New Chitose Airport or Shin-Sapporo Stn (free) → Furano → Biei → Asahikawa → Return at office

Farm Tomita's lavender fields, Biei's Blue Pond and the Patchwork Road — the classic route packed with Hokkaido's most iconic views. Optional stop at Asahiyama Zoo. Total distance approx. 400 km.

Lavender Blue Pond Patchwork Road Asahiyama Zoo
📖 See the 3-day model plan →
2
Eastern Hokkaido & Shiretoko
World Heritage Route
🗓 6 days / 5 nights ・ May–Oct recommended
New Chitose Airport or Shin-Sapporo Stn (free) → Obihiro → Kushiro Wetlands → Shiretoko → Abashiri → Return at office

A grand loop of Hokkaido's wild east: the Shiretoko World Natural Heritage peninsula, Obihiro's butadon pork bowls, the Kushiro Wetlands and Abashiri's winter drift ice. Total distance approx. 1,200 km — you'll feel just how vast Hokkaido is.

Shiretoko World Heritage Kushiro Wetlands Obihiro butadon Abashiri drift ice
📖 See the 6-day World Heritage plan →
3
Southern Hokkaido & Hakodate
Night View & Onsen Route
🗓 4 days / 3 nights ・ popular year-round
New Chitose Airport or Shin-Sapporo Stn (free) → Lake Toya → Hakodate → Onuma Park → Noboribetsu → Return at office

Mt. Hakodate's world-famous night view, lakeside Toya, and Noboribetsu Onsen's "Hell Valley" hot springs. Stroll historic Hakodate, hit the morning market and feast on seafood. Total distance approx. 500 km.

Mt. Hakodate night view Lake Toya Noboribetsu Onsen Hakodate morning market
4
Northern Hokkaido
Cape Soya Challenge Route
🗓 5 days / 4 nights ・ Jun–Sep recommended
New Chitose Airport or Shin-Sapporo Stn (free) → Asahikawa → Wakkanai → Rebun & Rishiri → Biei → Return at office

The great run north to Cape Soya, the northernmost point of Japan. From Wakkanai, take a ferry side-trip to Rebun Island and the "Rishiri Fuji" volcano. Feel the overwhelming scale of the north. Total distance approx. 1,000 km.

Cape Soya (northernmost) Rebun Island Mt. Rishiri Grand north run

Frequently Asked Questions

help_outlineCan I rent with a foreign driver's license?

Yes. Visitors from most countries need an International Driving Permit (IDP) under the 1949 Geneva Convention, together with your home-country license and passport. Licenses from France, Germany, Switzerland, Belgium, Monaco, Estonia and Taiwan are valid with an official Japanese translation (available through JAF) instead of an IDP. The IDP must be obtained in your home country before travel.

help_outlineCan I book and communicate in English?

Yes — please email us at info@green-rentacar.net in English. Phone support is available in Japanese only.

help_outlineWhat license do I need to drive HAPPY1?

A regular car license (AT-only is fine) is enough. Drivers must be 18 to under 75, with at least 1 year since obtaining their license.

help_outlineHow do I get to the shop from New Chitose Airport?

Our shop (Shiroishi-ku, Sapporo) is about 35 minutes by car from New Chitose Airport. We offer free pickup at Shin-Sapporo Station (direct trains from the airport and central Sapporo), and we can also deliver the campervan to New Chitose Airport (¥16,500 per trip).

help_outlineCan we bring our pet?

Yes! Up to 2 small dogs, or 1 medium/large dog. Please let us know in advance. Pets should be in a cage while driving and on a leash outside the vehicle.

help_outlineIs it really comfortable to sleep in?

The seats convert into full-flat beds for up to 4 adults. Sleeping bags, blankets and mattresses are provided for everyone free of charge, and the FF heater keeps the cabin toasty in winter.

help_outlineIs insurance included?

Basic automobile insurance (unlimited bodily injury & property damage; property deductible ¥150,000) is included in the base price. In the event of an accident, you pay the deductible (¥150,000).

help_outlineWhen do cancellation fees apply?

From 29 days before departure: 29–15 days before 30%, 14–8 days 50%, 7–1 days 80%, same day 100%.

help_outlineIt's our first campervan — will we manage?

Absolutely. Every rental starts with a free 30-minute orientation covering driving, bed setup, the heater and more.

help_outlineWhere can we park overnight?

We recommend RV parks and auto campsites, which are common all over Hokkaido. Our staff will gladly suggest good spots when you depart.

help_outlineDo you rent camping or cooking equipment (options)?

[Free standard equipment] sleeping bags, blankets, comfort mattresses, car fridge
[Paid options] induction cooktop (¥550), electric kettle (¥1,100), portable power station (¥3,300), folding table & lounge chairs (¥1,100 each)

Key Terms

Cancellation policy
  • 30+ days before the start date: no cancellation fee
  • 29–15 days before: 30% of the total
  • 14–8 days before: 50% of the total
  • 7–1 days before: 80% of the total
  • Same day / no-show: 100% of the total
Coverage & liability
  • Basic insurance (unlimited bodily injury & property damage / personal injury ¥30,000,000 per person) on every vehicle
  • Property-damage deductible: ¥150,000 per accident
  • NOC (non-operation charge): drivable ¥150,000 / not drivable (towed) ¥200,000
License requirements
  • A regular car license (AT-only OK) is sufficient
  • Drivers must be 18 to under 75 with 1+ year since obtaining their license
  • All drivers must bring their license on the day — international visitors also need an IDP (1949 Geneva Convention) or official Japanese translation, plus passport
⚠ Notes for driving in Hokkaido
  • Winter driving: studless snow tires fitted as standard. Avoid sudden steering and braking on snow/ice
  • Wildlife: beware of deer collisions when driving at night in eastern/northern Hokkaido
  • No smoking in the vehicle — cleaning fees apply
  • No toilet on board: use roadside stations, campsites and service areas
  • Fuel: please return with a full tank
