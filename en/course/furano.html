DAY 1

Pick up at the airport, head north to Biei

New Chitose Airport → Asahikawa → Biei

directions_carApprox. 170 km ／ about 2.5 hours' driving (excluding sightseeing)
  • ✈️
    AM

    Pick up your campervan at New Chitose Airport → set off

    We deliver the HAPPY1 directly to New Chitose Airport. Land, hop in, and your Hokkaido trip begins — no detour to our office, zero time lost.

    💡 Request airport delivery when booking ── we also drop you at Shin-Sapporo Station after your return
  • 🍜
    NOON

    Asahikawa ramen for lunch

    Asahikawa is the birthplace of one of Hokkaido's three great ramen styles — a rich soy-sauce broth. Long-standing favorites include Hachiya and Santouka.

    💡 The van has a kitchen, so stocking up at a supermarket and cooking your own meals is great too
  • 🌄
    PM

    Drive Biei's "Patchwork Road"

    Rolling hills quilted with wheat, potato and beet fields. Cruise past famous spots like the "Ken & Mary Tree" and the "Seven Stars Tree".

  • 🌅
    EVE

    Sunset at the "Philosophy Tree" area

    The lone leaning tree against the sunset is breathtaking — one of the most romantic scenes on the route.

  • 🌙
    NIGHT

    Overnight at the "Biei Shirogane Birke" roadside station

    Roadside stations (michi-no-eki) around Biei are popular overnight spots for campervans. Quiet surroundings and, on clear nights, a sky full of stars.

    💡 Nights get chilly, but with heating and cooling on board, HAPPY1 stays comfortable
🚐

Night 1 ── roadside station or RV park (around Biei)

The "Biei Shirogane Birke" roadside station has 24-hour toilets. Nearby RV parks add power hookups and showers (approx. ¥1,000–3,000).

DAY 2

A full day in lavender-covered Furano

Biei → Furano → Farm Tomita → Mt. Tokachi area

directions_carApprox. 60 km ／ a slow sightseeing day
  • 🌄
    MORNING

    Morning stroll at Biei's "Blue Pond"

    Near Shirogane Onsen, the Blue Pond glows a surreal cobalt blue in the morning light. Go early to beat the crowds — it's less than 10 minutes from where you slept. The dead larch trunks standing in the water change color completely with the weather and the angle of the light.

    💡 Parking is paid ── the walking path around the pond takes about 15 minutes
  • 💧
    MORNING

    Shirahige Falls & the Blue River Bridge

    About 5 minutes by car from the Blue Pond. Groundwater seeps straight out of the cliff face and drops into the Biei River — a rare "subsurface-flow waterfall" whose pale blue stream feeds the Blue Pond downstream. View it from the Blue River Bridge, a short walk from the car park.

    💡 Blue Pond, Shirahige Falls and Shirogane Onsen all sit within a 3 km radius
  • 🌸
    AM–PM

    Farm Tomita (Furano)

    Hokkaido's most famous lavender farm. Lavender peaks in early-to-mid July, alongside poppies, sunflowers and rainbow-striped flower fields. Don't miss the lavender soft-serve ice cream.

    💡 Free entry ── take your time wandering the vast flower fields together
  • 🧀
    NOON

    Furano Cheese Factory, or cook lunch in the van

    Watch cheese being made and enjoy tastings at the Furano Cheese Factory. Or grab a local jingisukan (grilled lamb) set from a roadside station and picnic somewhere scenic.

  • 🌋
    PM

    Drive up to the Tokachi-dake Bogakudai lookout

    You can drive all the way to this lookout at 930 m elevation. The contrast of stark volcanic terrain and green hills is spectacular — on clear days you can see all the way to Furano town.

  • ♨️
    EVE

    Soak at Shirogane Onsen

    Hot springs at the foot of Mt. Tokachi with several day-use bath houses. Melt away the day's driving (approx. ¥600–1,000).

  • 🍷
    NIGHT

    Overnight in the Furano area

    The "Minami Furano" roadside station and the area around the Furano Winery are convenient. Toast the evening with Furano wine and local ingredients — dinner for just the two of you.

🚐

Night 2 ── "Minami Furano" roadside station or an RV park in Furano

"RV Park Furano" has power and showers (reservation recommended). Roadside stations are free — just park considerately.

DAY 3

Homeward via a stop in Sapporo

Furano → Sapporo → return at office → Shin-Sapporo Station drop-off

directions_carApprox. 160 km ／ adjust to your flight time
  • 🌫
    MORNING

    Misty morning drive out of Furano

    Early mornings in the Furano basin often bring dreamy fog. Wake up slowly and brew a coffee — mornings like this are what campervan travel is all about.

  • 🍈
    MORNING

    Buy a Furano melon at a roadside stand

    Before leaving Furano, stop at one of the farm stands along the road. In summer they start from around ¥1,200 a melon. Chill it in the van's fridge on the way home — cheaper than the airport gift shops, and you get to pick one at its peak.

    💡 Many stands also ship ── use courier delivery if you're worried about it surviving the flight
  • 🏙
    AM–NOON

    Sightseeing in Sapporo (Clock Tower, Odori Park)

    Furano to Sapporo is about 1 hour 40 minutes. Stroll the Clock Tower, Odori Park and Susukino. For lunch, try Sapporo miso ramen or soup curry.

    💡 Central Sapporo parking is paid ── park in the suburbs and take the subway
  • 🛒
    PM

    Souvenir shopping

    New Chitose Airport has the best selection: Rokkatei, Shiroi Koibito, Royce' and every famous Hokkaido treat, all in one place.

  • 🚐
    RETURN

    Return the van at Green Rent a Car Hokkaido

    After you return the vehicle, we'll drive you to Shin-Sapporo Station with easy subway and JR connections. Safe travels home!
