DAY 1

From the airport south to Tokachi & Obihiro

New Chitose Airport → Obihiro (overnight)

directions_carTake the Doto Expressway to Obihiro
  • ✈️
    AM

    Pick up your HAPPY1 at New Chitose Airport → set off

    We deliver the campervan directly to New Chitose Airport. Land, load up and go — no detour to our office needed.

    💡 HAPPY1 has plenty of storage for 5 nights' worth of luggage ── pack with room to spare
  • 🌽
    NOON

    Lunch at Tokachi SA or a roadside station

    Cruise the Doto Expressway toward Obihiro. Stop at Tokachi SA or the "Jukai Road Hidaka" roadside station for lunch featuring Hokkaido potatoes and sweet corn.

  • 🍰
    PM

    Obihiro town + the original Rokkatei shop

    Tokachi-Obihiro is the heartland of Hokkaido farming. At the Rokkatei flagship store you can buy the famous Marusei butter sandwich cookies and relax in the café.

  • 🥩
    DINNER

    Obihiro's signature "butadon" pork bowl

    Sweet-savory charcoal-grilled pork over rice — simple and unforgettable. Historic favorites like Pancho and Hageten are all over town.

  • 🌙
    NIGHT

    Overnight at a roadside station or RV park in Obihiro

    The "Otofuke" roadside station is close to Obihiro with excellent facilities. RV parks add power hookups and showers.

🚐

Night 1 ── "Otofuke Natsuzora-no-Furusato" roadside station or an Obihiro RV park

24-hour toilets available. Big nature days start tomorrow, so rest up tonight.

DAY 2

To Kushiro Wetlands, Japan's largest marshland

Obihiro → Kushiro Wetlands → Kushiro (overnight)

directions_carA day of wetlands and wildlife
  • 🌫
    MORNING

    Morning mist from the Hosooka Observatory

    Just after sunrise, fog drifts across the wetlands in an otherworldly scene. Because you slept nearby, you'll have it to yourselves before the tour buses arrive. You may even spot red-crowned cranes.

    💡 The mist is most beautiful around 6–8 am ── bring warm layers
  • 🦌
    AM

    Walk Kushiro Shitsugen National Park

    The boardwalk trail from the Onnenai Visitor Center takes you right through the marsh — with chances to meet red-crowned cranes, Ezo deer and northern foxes.

  • 🛶
    PM

    Canoe the Kushiro River (optional)

    Popular guided canoe tours drift down the calm Kushiro River. The wetlands seen from the water are a completely different spectacle (advance booking required, approx. ¥6,000–10,000 per person).

  • 🦀
    DINNER

    Robatayaki seafood in Kushiro

    Kushiro is one of Hokkaido's great fishing ports. At a "robatayaki" grill, feast on hairy crab, sanma and hokki clams cooked over charcoal.

  • 🌙
    NIGHT

    Overnight near Kushiro

    Stay on the Kushiro side ahead of tomorrow's run to Shiretoko. The seaside "Shiranuka Koitoi" roadside station has lovely views.

🚐

Night 2 ── "Shiranuka Koitoi" roadside station or a Kushiro RV park

A great seaside location — fall asleep to the sound of the waves.

DAY 3

Arrival at Shiretoko, World Natural Heritage

Kushiro → Shiretoko (Shari / Utoro) (overnight)

directions_carThe scenic route along the Sea of Okhotsk
  • 🌊
    AM

    Drive the Okhotsk coast to Shiretoko

    Head north from Kushiro on Route 244, hugging the Sea of Okhotsk. Endless sea and open moorland alternate in a quintessentially Hokkaido landscape.

  • 🐻
    PM

    Walk the elevated boardwalk at the Shiretoko Five Lakes

    A trail linking five lakes deep in the primeval forest of the World Heritage peninsula. The free elevated boardwalk lets you look out over brown-bear country in complete safety — an unforgettable experience.

    💡 During peak bear season (May–July) parts of the ground trails close ── check the Shiretoko Foundation's official updates
  • 🚢
    EVE

    Shiretoko sightseeing cruise (optional)

    The sea cliffs of Shiretoko seen from the water are on another scale entirely. You may spot brown bears walking the cliff edges, or dolphins and orcas offshore (2–3 hour cruises, approx. ¥5,000–8,000 per person, booking recommended).

  • ♨️
    EVE

    Kamuiwakka hot falls (season/conditions permitting)

    A hidden waterfall of naturally hot spring water you can climb like a mountain stream. Access is limited to certain periods and conditions — check with the Shiretoko Foundation in advance.

  • 🌙
    NIGHT

    Overnight in the Utoro district

    Utoro, the gateway to Shiretoko, has a well-equipped roadside station with convenience stores and hot-spring baths nearby. Rest well before tomorrow's crossing of the Shiretoko Pass.

🚐

Night 3 ── "Utoro Shirietoku" roadside station or an Utoro RV park

The perfect base for exploring Shiretoko, with hot-spring facilities close by.

DAY 4

Over the Shiretoko Pass, then north across the east

Shiretoko (Utoro) → Shiretoko Pass → Rausu → Abashiri (overnight)

directions_carOne of Hokkaido's greatest mountain-pass drives
  • ⛰
    AM

    Drive the Shiretoko Pass (elev. 738 m)

    The trans-Shiretoko road from Utoro to Rausu is one of Hokkaido's most spectacular drives — Mt. Rausu towering overhead, Kunashiri Island to your right and the Sea of Okhotsk to your left.

    💡 Closed in winter (late Oct–mid May) ── the autumn foliage season (Sep–early Oct) is especially beautiful
  • 🐋
    AM–NOON

    Whale watching from Rausu (optional)

    Orcas and sperm whales frequent the seas off Rausu — Hokkaido's premier whale-watching waters, with high encounter rates in summer and autumn (approx. ¥8,000–12,000 per person, booking required).

  • 🦐
    NOON

    Kombu & seafood lunch in Rausu

    Rausu is home to the prized "Rausu kombu" kelp. The "Shiretoko Rausu" roadside station sells ultra-fresh seafood and serves it too.

  • 🐟
    PM

    Shibetsu Salmon Science Museum (side stop)

    During the autumn salmon run you can watch salmon swimming upriver — fascinating for kids and adults alike.

  • 🌙
    NIGHT

    On to Abashiri for the night

    Stay near the city ready for tomorrow's sightseeing. The "Ryuhyo Kaido Abashiri" roadside station sits right on the Sea of Okhotsk.

🚐

Night 4 ── "Ryuhyo Kaido Abashiri" roadside station or an Abashiri RV park

A prime spot facing the Sea of Okhotsk — in winter it's a base for drift-ice tours.

DAY 5

Abashiri sights, then Japan's largest lagoon

Abashiri → Kitami → Lake Saroma → Asahikawa area (overnight)

directions_carA day of Okhotsk history and scenery
  • 🏛
    AM

    Abashiri Prison Museum

    An open-air museum preserving Meiji-era prison buildings, many designated Important Cultural Properties — a vivid window into Hokkaido's pioneer history.

  • 🌾
    AM–NOON

    Coral grass at Lake Notoro (Sep–Oct)

    In autumn the lakeshore turns crimson with coral grass. The contrast of the scarlet carpet and blue water is stunning.

  • 🌊
    PM

    Lake Saroma lookout

    Japan's largest lagoon. At the Wakka Wildflower Reserve you can walk the narrow sand spit between lake and sea.

  • 🍦
    PM

    Kitami Mint Memorial Museum + mint soft-serve

    Kitami once produced most of the world's mint. The signature mint soft-serve is wonderfully refreshing.

  • 🌙
    NIGHT

    Overnight at a roadside station toward Asahikawa

    Position yourselves toward central Hokkaido for tomorrow's return — aim for the Biei/Asahikawa area, e.g. the "Biei Oka-no-Kura" roadside station.

🚐

Night 5 ── "Biei Oka-no-Kura" roadside station or an RV park near Asahikawa

Staying here lets you squeeze in Biei or Furano on the final day.

DAY 6

Savor the last stretch back to Sapporo

Asahikawa area → Sapporo → return at office → Shin-Sapporo Station drop-off

directions_carAdjust to your flight time
  • 🌄
    MORNING

    A morning walk in Biei or Furano (if time allows)

    On the way back you can stop at the hills of Biei or Furano's lavender fields. The hills in early-morning light are a farewell gift from Hokkaido.

  • 🛒
    AM–NOON

    Final sights & souvenirs in Sapporo

    Stroll the Clock Tower and Odori Park, or browse the shopping mall at New Chitose Airport at leisure.

    💡 The airport's "Hokkaido Dosanko Plaza" gathers specialties from every town in Hokkaido ── the single best souvenir stop
  • 🚐
    RETURN

    Return the van at Green Rent a Car Hokkaido

    After you return the vehicle we'll drive you to Shin-Sapporo Station (please request in advance). Six days, 1,200 km — what a trip. Safe travels home!
